# P01 — ONE CONNECTED BUSINESS JOURNEY
**"The Controller sets the rules of the books" — Kunes Ford of Delavan, first R1 month.**
1. **Govern the structure (S009).** Maria (Controller) opens COA Governance, filters the accounts today's statements flag as "Unsupported classification," maps 50100 Cost of Parts Sold and 50200 Delivery Expense to their statement lines with a reason. The statements preview shows Gross Profit forming. *Consequence: the Golden R0 limitation banner retires for mapped accounts; every mapping is audited.*
2. **Add the analysis lens (S011 — design now, build post-SES-3).** She defines type "Campaign" with values SPRING-SVC and FALL-TIRE. From now on, service-promo accruals can be tagged and sliced in GL Inquiry without new accounts.
3. **Mark the group's elimination home (S003).** The group adds "Kunes Eliminations LLC"; Maria flags it as an elimination entity. It refuses stores — visible on the org tree — so intercompany cleanup (R5) has a governed home before a single elimination posts.
4. **Automate the standard entries (S032).** She builds template RT-RENT (balanced, fixed amounts) and RT-UTIL-ACCR (auto-reverse). "Generate for 2026-09" creates draft journals through the same journal lifecycle as hand-typed entries; she posts them under her existing permissions. The accrual's reversing draft appears dated Oct 1, waiting for a human.
5. **Close the month (S008).** Sep ends: soft close blocks operational sources while she posts final GJ adjustments; drafts worklist reaches zero; hard close with the typed-name ceremony. The database itself now refuses September. In October an auditor asks about a late invoice — reopen is a reasoned, audited ceremony, not a favor.
**Exception threads woven through the journey (each demonstrated in the design artifact):** hard-close attempt with open drafts (blocked, worklist); generation into a soft-closed period (refused with the period rule); flagging an entity that owns stores (422 with the list); reclassifying an account with history (effective-period rule); duplicate generation (idempotent).
**Completion state:** governed COA feeding correct statements, dimension tagging ready, elimination home established, standard entries automated, and a month that is provably, enforceably closed.
